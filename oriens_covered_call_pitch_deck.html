Slide 3 / The Problem

The current flow is fragmented, manual, and hard to manage at scale.

Portfolio blind spots

Traders can see stock holdings and option trades separately, but not the exact covered mapping, uncovered value, or assignment exposure in one decisive view.

Weak action priority

Most platforms show prices, not decision pressure. They do not surface what is nearing strike, what expires this week, or what should be rolled first.

Existing tools fall short

Broker terminals are execution-first. Screeners are idea-first. Reporting tools are portfolio-first. None combine these into one covered-call operating console.

Slide 4 / The Solution

ORIENS turns covered-call trading into a managed system.

  • Dedicated covered-call dashboard: holdings, sold calls, premium, expiry pressure, and roll candidates.
  • Live broker-linked options tools already exist: chain, OI, orderbook, positions, tradebook, and strategy workflows.
  • Rule-ready architecture for strike proposals: %OTM, yield-on-margin, utilization, expiry filters, and later Greeks-based ranking.
  • Execution surface for manual and automated trade handling without switching terminals all day.

UX Audit Snapshot

Core flow design priorities

Weak hierarchy to avoid

Price-heavy dashboards with no action priority, no coverage view, and no expiry urgency are not enough for covered-call management.

Feedback that must exist

Roll suggestions, expiry warnings, uncovered holdings, and premium-ledger confirmation should always be visible.

Error/empty states to build

No calls sold, partial coverage, missed expiry mapping, stale broker sync, and low-liquidity contracts must have explicit state design.
